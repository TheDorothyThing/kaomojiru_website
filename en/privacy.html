Kaomojiru - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 30, 2026

Welcome to Kaomojiru (the “App,” “we,” or “us”), developed and operated by Dorothy (the “Developer”). We care about your privacy, and this Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your data.

If you have any questions about this Policy, please contact us at thedorothything@gmail.com.

1. Information We Collect

1-1 Information We Do Not Collect

The core functionality of Kaomojiru — browsing kaomoji, searching, filtering, saving favorites, and inputting kaomoji via the custom keyboard — runs entirely on your device, locally. We do not:

  • Require you to create an account
  • Collect your name, email address (unless you voluntarily email us), phone number, or other personally identifiable information
  • Upload your saved favorite kaomoji, search history, custom kaomoji content, language settings, or appearance preferences to our servers or any cloud service
  • Share or publicly display any of your usage content with other users

The favorite kaomoji you save (stored locally in a SQLite database), your search and filter history, and your preferences (language, dark mode, vibration, etc.) are stored only on your device, and shared between the “Kaomojiru” main app and the “Kaomojiru” custom keyboard extension via App Group technology. None of this data is transmitted to the Developer or any third party.

1-2 Content You Enter Yourself

The App allows you to create “custom kaomoji” of up to 12 characters, which you enter yourself. Please note:

  • This content is stored only on your device; we cannot access it
  • Due to technical limitations of the system, we cannot restrict or filter the text you enter. You are responsible for any content you input, and should avoid entering sensitive personal information or inappropriate content (see “User Conduct” in our Terms of Service)

1-3 Information Collected by Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services to provide advertising and related functionality. These services may independently collect data:

Google AdMob (Advertising Service)

The free version of the App displays banner ads, and all users may optionally watch rewarded video ads in exchange for additional favorite slots. These ads are served by Google AdMob. AdMob and its advertising partners may collect the following types of data to serve ads, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud:

  • Device advertising identifiers (e.g., Apple’s IDFA, subject to your consent via “App Tracking Transparency”)
  • IP address (which may be used to infer approximate location)
  • Device information (e.g., device model, operating system version)
  • Ad interaction data (e.g., impressions, clicks, video completion rate)

This data is processed by Google and its advertising partners under their respective privacy policies, and is not directly collected or stored by the Developer. Please refer to:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob Partners and Data Usage: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/9012903

Important: The custom keyboard extension does not contain any ads and does not collect or transmit any data. Ads only appear in the main Kaomojiru app.

Apple App Store / In-App Purchase

If you purchase “Kaomojiru PRO,” this transaction is processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not access, collect, or store your payment information (such as credit card numbers). Apple may process your purchase records under its own privacy policy; see Apple’s Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

1-4 Information You Voluntarily Provide

If you contact us via the “Send Feedback” feature or by emailing thedorothything@gmail.com directly, we will receive your email address and the content of your message. This is used solely to respond to your inquiry or feedback, and will not be used for any other purpose or shared with third parties.

2. App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

In accordance with Apple’s requirements, if the App or its advertising partners need to access your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA) for cross-app tracking or targeted advertising, the system will display a permission prompt asking for your consent. You may choose “Allow” or “Ask App Not to Track.” This choice does not affect your ability to use the App’s core functionality (browsing, favoriting, and inputting kaomoji via the keyboard). Even if you decline, you may still see ads — they will simply be less personalized.

You can change this permission at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking on your device.

3. Data Security

Since the App’s core data is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to any external server, the security of your favorites and settings primarily depends on the security of your device itself (e.g., device passcode, iCloud backup encryption provided by Apple). We recommend you take appropriate measures to protect access to your device.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Since the App does not store your personal data on any server, you can delete locally stored data at any time by:

  • Manually deleting individual favorite kaomoji within the App
  • Removing (uninstalling) the App from your device, which will delete all locally stored data, including your favorites list, search history, and preferences

For data collected by third-party advertising services (such as Google AdMob), please refer to that service’s own retention and deletion policies.

5. Children’s Privacy

The App is not specifically designed for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Since the App itself does not collect any personally identifiable information, this risk is significantly reduced; however, advertising services may be subject to their own policies. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can address it.

6. Your Rights

Because the App is designed around local data storage, you retain full control over your own data: you may view, edit, or delete your saved favorites within the App at any time, and you may permanently erase all locally stored data by uninstalling the App.

If you have questions about data collected by third-party services (such as Google AdMob), or wish to exercise rights such as data access or deletion, please contact that third party directly, or refer to the links provided in Section 1-3.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App’s features or applicable legal requirements. If we make material changes, we will notify you by updating the “Terms of Service / Privacy Policy” page within the App, and by updating the “Last Updated” date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically for the latest information.

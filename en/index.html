There are a few ways. Open the iOS Settings app and go to General > Keyboard > Keyboards > Add New Keyboard…, then select Kaomojiru under Third-Party Keyboards. You can also tap Keyboard Settings… at the top of the menu when switching keyboards. Once the Settings app opens, go to Keyboards > Add New Keyboard… and follow the same steps.

You can also set it up from the Kaomojiru app. Go to the Settings tab, choose How to Set Up the Keyboard, scroll to the bottom, and tap Go to Settings. Then tap Keyboards and turn on Kaomojiru.
